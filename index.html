物流的基本概念What logistics is

物流（logistics）是把「對的東西」在「對的時間」、以「對的數量和狀態」送到「對的地點」所需的一連串活動。它不只是運送：事前的規劃、中間的倉儲與庫存管理、事後的回收都包含在內。說得更精確：物流是貨物從來源到使用地點的移動與儲存的規劃與控制，涵蓋運輸、倉儲、庫存，以及追蹤這些活動的資訊。

Logistics is the chain of activities that gets the right item to the right place, at the right time, in the right quantity and condition. It is more than transport: the planning before, the warehousing and inventory management in between, and the returns after are all part of it. More precisely, logistics is the planning and control of the movement and storage of goods from their source to the point of use, covering transport, warehousing, inventory and the information that tracks them.

物流包含哪些活動What logistics covers

規劃與預測Planning and forecasting

估計未來需要多少、什麼時候需要、放在哪裡。estimating how much will be needed, when and where.

採購與下單Purchasing

向供應商下採購單，並追蹤出貨日期與交期。placing purchase orders with suppliers and tracking shipping dates and lead times.

運輸Transport

把貨物從供應商送到倉庫，再送到需要的地點。moving goods from the supplier to the warehouse and on to where they are needed.

倉儲Warehousing

收貨、上架、保管與揀貨出庫。receiving, put-away, storage and picking.

庫存管理Inventory management

決定每個地點要放多少存貨，既不缺貨也不囤積。deciding how much stock each location holds, avoiding both shortages and excess.

退貨與逆物流Returns and reverse logistics

把不良品或多餘的貨收回、維修、再利用或報廢。collecting defective or surplus goods for repair, reuse or scrapping.

物流要平衡的三件事The three things logistics balances

缺貨Shortages

存貨放太少，維修和出貨都要等；服務水準下降。Too little stock means waiting repairs and late shipments; the service level drops.

庫存成本Inventory cost

存貨放太多，積壓資金與倉儲空間，零件還可能過時。Too much stock ties up money and warehouse space, and parts can become obsolete.

交期Lead time

供應商出貨與運送要多久，決定了必須提前多久下單。How long the supplier and the transport take decides how far ahead an order must be placed.

物流的日常工作，就是在服務水準、庫存成本與交期之間取得平衡。Day-to-day logistics is the balance between service level, inventory cost and lead time.

服務物流是什麼What service logistics is

服務物流（售後服務物流）的任務，是讓每一件維修所需的備用零件在正確的時間、以正確的數量出現在維修據點，並把更換下來的不良品收回處理。在泛亞太區，這件事由總部的 QSBU 規劃、泛亞太區區域辦公室（PAP RO）執行，再由各國子公司（NS）在當地完成。

Service (after-sales) logistics makes sure every spare part a repair needs is at the service site at the right time and in the right quantity, and that the replaced defective parts are collected and dealt with. In Pan Asia Pacific, QSBU at headquarters plans this work, the Pan Asia Pacific regional office (PAP RO) carries it out, and the nation subsidiaries (NS) complete it locally.

與生產物流的差異How it differs from production logistics

生產物流Production logistics
  • 目的Purpose：把產品做出來並賣出去。: make the product and sell it.
  • 需求來源Demand：依生產計畫，可事先排定。: the production plan; it can be scheduled in advance.
  • 數量與種類Quantity and variety：大批量，種類相對集中。: large batches, relatively few items.
  • 時間要求Time pressure：依排程交貨。: delivery to a schedule.
  • 期間Duration：產品停產即結束。: ends when production stops.
  • 流向Direction：單向，零件 → 工廠 → 成品 → 客戶。: one way, parts → factory → product → customer.
服務物流Service logistics
  • 目的Purpose：讓已售出的產品能持續被維修、繼續使用。: keep products already sold repairable and in use.
  • 需求來源Demand：由故障發生決定，不確定、不規則。: failures; uncertain and irregular.
  • 數量與種類Quantity and variety：小批量，料號非常多（長尾）。: small quantities across a very long list of part numbers.
  • 時間要求Time pressure：維修在等料，就是客戶在等，講求速度。: a repair waiting for a part is a customer waiting; speed matters.
  • 期間Duration：停產後仍須供應多年，因此要在停產前做最後採購。: continues for years after production ends, so a last buy is made before it stops.
  • 流向Direction：雙向，良品送出、不良品收回。: two ways, good parts out and defective parts back.

備用零件的特性Characteristics of spare parts

備用零件（備品）是為了維修而準備的零件。它的需求和成品很不一樣：不確定、數量小、種類多，而且要在產品停產後持續供應好幾年。這些特性決定了服務物流的做法。

Spare parts are the parts kept for repairs. Their demand is unlike that for finished products: uncertain, small in quantity, very wide in variety, and it continues for years after a product stops being made. These characteristics shape how service logistics works.

需求特性Demand characteristics

間歇且不規則Intermittent and irregular

需求來自故障，受故障率與市場上仍在使用的機器數量影響，無法像生產一樣排定。demand comes from failures and depends on failure rates and how many units are still in use; it cannot be scheduled like production.

長尾A long tail

料號數量非常多，多數流動緩慢、少數流動快速；大多數料號一年只用到幾件。there are a great many part numbers; most move slowly and a few move fast, and most are used only a few times a year.

依機種而異Model-specific

同一功能的零件（例如鍵盤、主機板），在不同機種、有時不同國家，都是不同料號。The same function (a keyboard, a mainboard) has a different part number for each model, and sometimes for each country.

時間壓力Time pressure

缺一個零件，一台機器就修不好；待料（HFP）的時間直接影響客戶滿意度（CSAT）。one missing part stops a repair; time spent waiting for parts (HFP) directly affects customer satisfaction (CSAT).

生命週期長A long life

產品停產（EOL）後仍要供應維修用料，因此有最後採購（LTB）與停產後採購（PPB1/2）的機制。parts must still be supplied after end of life (EOL), which is why there are last-time buys (LTB) and post-production buys (PPB1/2).

替代料Alternates

同一個位置可能有多個可互換的料號，須在 Service BOM 中維護。one position may have several interchangeable part numbers, which must be maintained in the Service BOM.

雙向流動Two-way flow

良品送到據點，更換下來的不良品依 RMA 流程退回。good parts go out to the sites and the replaced defective parts come back under the RMA process.

價值差異大A wide range of value

從螺絲到主機板都是備品，規劃時依類別分別處理。anything from a screw to a mainboard is a spare part, so planning treats the classes differently.

一個重要觀念：FRUA key concept: the FRU

FRU（Field Replaceable Unit，現場可更換單元）是維修時整個換掉的單位。「現場」指工廠以外的地方，例如維修中心或到府服務時的客人家裡；「可更換」指不需要生產設備，就能整個拆下、裝上新的。A FRU (field replaceable unit) is the unit swapped as a whole during a repair. 'Field' means anywhere outside the factory, such as a service centre or a customer's home; 'replaceable' means it can be taken out and a new one fitted without factory equipment.

是 FRUA FRU

電池、螢幕、鍵盤、硬碟、記憶體、風扇、變壓器等。技師打開機殼，鬆幾顆螺絲、拔下排線，就能整個換掉。Batteries, screens, keyboards, drives, memory, fans, adapters and the like. The technician opens the case, undoes a few screws and cables, and swaps the whole part.

不單獨當 FRUNot a FRU on its own

焊在主機板上的晶片、電容。要換得用焊接設備，維修中心做不來也不划算，所以整片主機板當成一個 FRU 來換。Chips and capacitors soldered to the mainboard. Replacing them needs soldering equipment, which a service centre lacks and which would not pay, so the whole mainboard is swapped as one FRU.

修得快Faster repairs

以模組為單位整個換，機器很快就能交還。Swapping whole modules gets the unit back quickly.

品質穩Steady quality

換上的是完整的新模組，結果一致。A complete new module gives consistent results.

容易上手Easy to learn

技師不需要高深的電子維修能力。Technicians do not need advanced electronics repair skills.

哪些零件列為 FRU，決定了維修中心能修什麼、要備什麼；備品清單（SPL）列的就是這些單位，備品規劃也以 FRU 為單位。一台筆電的生產清單約有 1,200 項零件，整理成 SPL 後約剩 120 項。Which parts count as FRUs decides what a service centre can fix and what it must stock; the spare parts list (SPL) lists these units, and spare-parts planning works at the FRU level. A notebook's production list has about 1,200 items; the SPL keeps about 120.

ODM Original Design Manufacturer

OEM Original Equipment Manufacturer

原廠委託製造。品牌商提供產品設計與規格，代工廠依設計生產，產品以品牌商之品牌銷售。The brand provides the design and specification; the manufacturer builds to that design, and the product is sold under the brand's name.

EMS Electronics Manufacturing Services

電子製造服務。受託進行電子產品之製造、組裝與測試，通常不負責產品設計，且同時服務多個品牌。本網站之例：鴻海。Contract manufacturing, assembly and testing of electronics, usually without product design, for many brands at once. Example on this site: Foxconn.

一件備品的旅程The journey of a spare part

一件備品從需求到維修的旅程，以及備品供應流程的基本概念；最後說明這條流程在 Acer 的組織中由誰負責、PAP 做哪些事。

The journey of a spare part from demand to repair and the basic supply flow; the section ends with who in the Acer organisation is responsible for each part of it and what PAP does.

  1. 1需求規劃Demand planning

    各國子公司（NS）提出需求，PAP 彙整預測並提交總部。The nation subsidiaries (NS) raise demand; PAP consolidates the forecast and submits it to headquarters.

  2. 2採購下單Purchasing

    PAP 依規劃向 ODM（如仁寶）下達採購單（PO）：在 CSS 建立，經 PLS 向 ODM 確認。PAP places purchase orders (POs) with the ODM, such as Compal, according to the plan: created in CSS and confirmed to the ODM in PLS.

  3. 3倉儲與料號Warehousing and part numbers

    管理中央倉與庫存；服務料號與 Service BOM 由總部 QSBU 維護。Manages the central warehouse and inventory; part numbers and the Service BOM are maintained by QSBU at headquarters.

  4. 4供應各國Supply to the countries

    料件供應至各國子公司；2026 年 12 月 1 日起經由 HSN Group。Parts are supplied to the nation subsidiaries; from 1 December 2026 through HSN Group.

  5. 5維修服務Repair

    各國服務中心與授權服務商（ASP）為客戶完成維修。Service centres and authorised service providers (ASPs) in each country complete the repair.

第 1 到第 4 階段由 PAP 負責，第 5 階段由各國執行。PAP is responsible for stages 1 to 4; stage 5 is carried out in each country.

實例：一片主機板Example: one mainboard

新加坡一位消費者的筆電無法開機。維修中心判定主機板故障，向新加坡子公司（ACS）的零件倉申請，卻剛好缺貨，案件只好標記為「待料」（HFP）：故障已查明，維修卻得等零件。A consumer in Singapore has a notebook that will not power on. The service centre finds a failed mainboard and asks the parts store of the Singapore subsidiary (ACS), which is out of stock, so the case is marked hold-for-part (HFP): the fault is known, but the repair waits for the part.

零件能不能及時送到，取決於前面幾個階段：上市前有沒有列為服務零件、需求有沒有估準、有沒有準時下單、ODM 有沒有提前備料、有沒有分到對的國家。Whether the part arrives in time depends on the earlier stages: whether it was made a service part before launch, whether demand was forecast well, whether the order went in on time, whether the ODM stocked ahead, and whether it went to the right country.

備品供應流程的基本概念The basic supply flow for spare parts

不論哪個品牌，備品供應都繞著同一條線運作：先估需求，再向供應商採購，進倉保管，依各地需求送出，最後在維修現場換上，並把換下來的不良品收回。Whatever the brand, spare-parts supply runs along the same line: estimate demand, buy from the supplier, hold the stock in a warehouse, ship it to where it is needed, fit it during the repair, and collect the defective part that was replaced.

1估需求Estimate demand

依維修量與故障率估計各地要多少備品。Estimate how many parts each location will need from repair volumes and failure rates.

2採購Buy

理想In theory
  • 知道每台機器的保固到期日與故障率。Each unit's warranty end date and failure rate are known.

SPL 定義流程SPL definition flow

一台筆電量產出貨前，要先把上千項的生產清單，精簡成各地維修真正用得到的零件，配好料號與價格：這份清單就是 SPL。下面先用一個故事走完全程，再看正式的流程圖與時程。

Before a notebook ships in volume, a production list of over a thousand items has to be cut down to the parts repairs really need, each with a part number and a price: that list is the SPL. The story below walks through the whole journey first, then the formal flow chart and timing.

插曲：零件出問題的時候Aside: when a part has a problem

有時同一批零件（例如主機板）故障率特別高，原因可能是原料不好，也可能是製程出錯。Acer 有三種做法：換一家供應商、向原廠商求償，或修改 SPL，替改良後的零件開新料號，讓各地之後訂料與維修都用沒問題的新零件，不和舊料混在一起。Sometimes one batch of parts, mainboards for instance, fails unusually often, because of poor raw material or a manufacturing error. Acer has three responses: switch supplier, claim compensation from the original vendor, or revise the SPL with a new part number for the improved part, so that orders and repairs from then on use the good part and never mix it with the old stock.

一句話：SPL 是在新機量產前，把上千項的生產清單精簡成各地維修真正需要的零件，配好料號與價格，讓每一台賣出去的筆電送修時都有零件可換。In one sentence: before a new model ships in volume, the SPL cuts a production list of over a thousand items down to the parts repairs really need, with part numbers and prices, so that every notebook sold has parts ready when it comes in for repair.

① 配置矩陣（Matrix table）① Matrix table

同一機種所有規格組合的總表：處理器、記憶體、硬碟、螢幕，再加上各國語言的鍵盤。照表核對，才不會漏備某一種配置的零件。A table of every configuration of the model: CPU, memory, storage, screen and keyboards for each language. Working from it ensures no configuration's parts are missed.

② 指定供應商零件清單（AVLC）② Assigned vendor list (AVLC)

重要零件（例如面板）由 Acer 指定可採購的廠商，ODM 必須照單購買，例如面板向友達（AUO）採購；零件好，之後送修就少。同一零件也常列兩家以上廠商，以免單一廠商斷料、也保有議價空間。不同廠商的版本外觀一樣，接頭、尺寸或韌體卻可能不同，所以要先確認哪些能互換、哪些要分開備料並給不同料號。For key parts such as panels Acer names the vendors the ODM must buy from, e.g. panels from AUO; better parts mean fewer repairs later. 流程為什麼這樣排Why the flow runs in this order

清單由兩邊分工完成Two sides share the work

ODM 最了解這台筆電用了哪些零件，所以依 Acer 的要求起草；QSBU 審核內容，再開料號、定價與發布。SPL 定案後，ODM 也照這份清單規劃維修零件的採購。The ODM knows best which parts the notebook uses, so it drafts the list to Acer's requirements; QSBU reviews it, then assigns part numbers, prices and releases it. Once the SPL is final, the ODM also plans its purchasing of service parts from it.

先定範圍，再談價格Scope first, price second

草稿先不含價格；要供應哪些零件定下來，報價與審核才有明確範圍。The draft has no prices; once the parts are fixed, quoting and review have a clear scope.

一律用宏碁料號Always Acer part numbers

料號就像零件的身分證字號。各家 ODM 的編號方式不同；統一用宏碁料號，各區、各國與各系統下單、出貨、庫存、帳務才對得起來。A part number is a part's ID. Each ODM numbers parts its own way; one set of Acer part numbers keeps ordering, shipping, stock and accounts aligned across regions, countries and systems.

加價有原因The markup has a reason

ODM 報價到賣給各區的價格之間，還有包裝、報關等處理成本，所以 QSBU 在報價上加一筆手續費（handling charge），訂出 service price，並反映服務體系採購、倉儲、分配與管理的投入。Between the ODM quote and the price to the regions sit packing, customs and other handling costs, so QSBU adds a handling charge to set the service price, also reflecting what the service organisation puts into buying, storing, allocating and managing parts.

殘值先談好Agree the carcass value early

換下的壞主機板仍有價值，可檢修翻新或回收材料；主機板單價高，殘值也可觀，由 HQ PM 在 C 樣品階段提供、QSBU 在 Markup Review 確認，退回時才能依約折抵。A failed mainboard still has value: it can be refurbished or recycled for materials. With mainboards this costly, the HQ PM supplies the carcass value at the C sample stage and QSBU confirms it in the markup review, so returns can be credited as agreed.

不合理就退回重報Send back what does not add up

審核發現價格偏高等不合理之處，就退回 ODM 從 SPL 草稿重新整理並報價，直到雙方都能接受。If the review finds prices out of line, it goes back to the ODM's SPL draft and is quoted again until both sides can accept it.

料號命名規則Part number naming rule

料號格式為 AA.BBBCD.EEE，共 10 個字元（小數點不算）。前兩碼 AA 是子類別代碼；第 6 碼表示區域（RO），第 7 碼表示該區域內的國家。

Part numbers follow AA.BBBCD.EEE. Dots are not counted: the 6th character is the RO code, the 7th is the country code, and the first two characters (AA) are the sub-category code.

第 6 碼與第 7 碼怎麼看How to read characters 6 and 7

D1G2.B38485S6A7.0809110
  1. 不計小數點，自左起依序為 D、G、B、8、8、S、A、0、0、1；第 6 碼為 S，第 7 碼為 A。Ignore the dots and count from the left: D, G, B, 8, 8, S, A, 0, 0, 1. The 6th character is S and the 7th is A.
  2. 第 6 碼＝區域代碼：S 為 AAP（亞太），T 為 TWN（台灣），C 為 ACCN（中國），E 為 EMEA，A 為 PA（美洲）。本例第 6 碼為 S，即 AAP 區之料號。Character 6 = which region. S is AAP (Asia Pacific), T is TWN (Taiwan), C is ACCN (China), E is EMEA and A is PA (Americas). The S here means this part number belongs to the AAP region.
  3. 第 7 碼＝該區域內之國別代碼，僅於第 6 碼為 S（AAP）時適用：A 為 ACA（澳洲），M 為 ASSB（馬來西亞），I 為 AIL（印度），完整對照見下表。本例第 7 碼為 A，即澳洲。Character 7 = which country in that region. It applies only when character 6 is S (AAP): A is ACA (Australia), M is ASSB (Malaysia), I is AIL (India), and so on; see the table below. The A here means Australia.
  4. 因此 DG.B88SA.001 為 AAP 區供澳洲使用之料號；同一料件供馬來西亞使用時，料號為 DG.B88SM.001，僅第 7 碼不同。So DG.B88SA.001 is the AAP-region part number for Australia; the same part for Malaysia is DG.B88SM.001, differing only in character 7.

一件備品的旅程The journey of a spare part

從需求到維修，PAP 服務物流負責其中的規劃、採購、倉儲與供應。From demand to repair: PAP service logistics handles the planning, purchasing, warehousing and supply along the way.

  1. 1需求規劃Demand planning

    各國子公司（NS）提出需求，PAP 彙整預測並提交總部。The nation subsidiaries (NS) raise demand; PAP consolidates the forecast and submits it to headquarters.

  2. 2採購下單Purchasing

    PAP 依規劃向 ODM（如仁寶）下達採購單（PO）。PAP places purchase orders (POs) with the ODM, such as Compal, according to the plan.

  3. 3倉儲與料號Warehousing and part numbers

    管理中央倉庫與庫存，維護 Service BOM 與服務料號。Manages the central warehouse and inventory, and maintains the Service BOM and part numbers.

  4. 4供應各國Supply to the countries

    料件供應至各國子公司；2026 年 12 月 1 日起經由 HSN Group。Parts are supplied to the nation subsidiaries; from 1 December 2026 through HSN Group.

  5. 5維修服務Repair

    各國服務中心與授權服務商（ASP）為客戶完成維修。Service centres and authorised service providers (ASPs) in each country complete the repair.

歷史由來Origins

1976 年 8 月，施振榮、葉紫華、邰中和、黃少華、林家和五位創辦人以新台幣 100 萬元在台北創立宏碁的前身 Multitech，以微處理器技術起家。1982 年推出的小教授二號（MicroProfessor-II），宏碁稱其為台灣第一部 8 位元家用電腦；1987 年改以源自拉丁文、意為「敏銳、能幹」的 Acer 作為品牌與公司名稱，並於 1988 年 4 月首次公開發行股票。1–5, 18

In August 1976 Stan Shih, Carolyn Yeh, Ken Tai, George Huang and Fred Lin founded Multitech, the forerunner of Acer, in Taipei with NT$1 million in capital and a focus on microprocessor technology. The MicroProfessor-II of 1982 is described by Acer as Taiwan's first 8-bit home computer; in 1987 the company adopted the name Acer, from the Latin for sharp and able, and it completed its initial public offering in April 1988.1–5, 18

2000 年底宣布的「第二次再造」把集團分為三家公司：研製服務部門於 2001 年 5 月分立為緯創資通，通訊與多媒體事業同年 12 月更名為明基，宏碁自此專注於自有品牌。其後透過 2007 年收購美國 Gateway（含 eMachines 品牌）與 2008 年取得歐洲 Packard Bell 75% 股權，擴大歐美市場。1, 7–9

The "second re-engineering" announced at the end of 2000 split the group in three: the design and manufacturing division became Wistron in May 2001, the communications and multimedia business was renamed BenQ that December, and Acer concentrated on its own brand. It then grew in the Americas and Europe by acquiring Gateway (with the eMachines brand) in 2007 and a 75% stake in Packard Bell in 2008.1, 7–9

2014 年陳俊聖出任總經理暨執行長（2017 年起兼任董事長），同年 8 月中國、台灣與亞太整合為泛亞太區營運；2016 年 3 月營運再分為核心事業與新事業，侯知遠（Andrew Hou）出任泛亞太區總裁。2026 年，宏碁集團邁入創立 50 週年。2–4, 10–15

Jason Chen became President and CEO in 2014 (and Chairman from 2017); from August that year China, Taiwan and Asia Pacific were grouped into one Pan Asia Pacific operation. In March 2016 the business was split into core and new businesses, and Andrew Hou was appointed President of Pan Asia Pacific. In 2026 the Acer group marks fifty years since its founding.2–4, 10–15

集團子公司Group subsidiaries

宏碁自 2019 年起陸續將內部事業分拆並推動上市櫃，媒體稱為「老虎隊」。每家公司以「歷史由來」與「業務與和 Acer 的關係」介紹；與 PAP 關係最密切的海柏特另有專章。

Since 2019 Acer has spun off internal businesses and taken them public, a group the press calls the "tiger team". Each company is introduced by its origins and its business and relationship with Acer; HSN, the subsidiary closest to PAP, has its own chapter.

子公司策略Subsidiary strategy

董事長暨執行長陳俊聖的作法，是在核心事業內找出趨勢、擴大規模，再以公開發行檢驗該事業的體質：由員工內部創業，宏碁保持多數持股。2022 年 7 月已有 7 家上市櫃子公司（展碁、宏碁資訊、安碁、智聯、建碁、宏碁智醫、海柏特）；2024 年 11 月博瑞達應材登錄興櫃，媒體稱為第 11 家；2025 年 11 月宣布投資日本 Opticon，稱為第 16 家。子公司數量因統計日期與是否計入興櫃而異，正式名單以宏碁投資人關係網頁為準。S1–S6 單一來源Single source

Chairman and CEO Jason Chen's approach is to spot a trend inside the core business, scale it up and then test the unit through a public offering: employees found the businesses internally while Acer keeps majority control. Seven subsidiaries were listed by July 2022 (Weblink, AEB, ACSI, AST, AOpen, Acer Medical and HSN); in November 2024 the press counted the 11th when BRD Materials joined the emerging board, and in November 2025 Acer announced its investment in Opticon (Japan) as the 16th. Counts differ by date and by whether emerging-board companies are included; the official list is on Acer's investor-relations page.S1–S6 單一來源Single source

How tenders work and why Uni-load is set up.

標案流程Tender process

  1. 1客戶（如政府機關、警政單位）發布標書（規格書）。The customer (e.g. a government agency or the police) issues a tender with a specification.
  2. 2各品牌依規格書投標，產品須符合規格要求。Brands bid against the specification; products must meet its requirements.
  3. 3得標後依規格委託 ODM（如仁寶 Compal）生產成品（FG）。The winning brand has an ODM (e.g. Compal) build finished goods (FG) to the specification.
  4. 4成品成本高於標案價格時，可採澳洲模式：向 ODM 採購半成品（螢幕、主機板），於當地採購 SSD、HDD 並自行組裝。If the FG cost exceeds the tender price, the Australian approach applies: semi-finished parts (screen, mainboard) are purchased from the ODM, SSDs and HDDs are sourced locally, and units are assembled locally.
  5. 5藉此使成本符合標案價格。This brings the cost within the tender price.

Uni-load 的目的Purpose of Uni-load

  • Uni-load 主要用於滿足當地客戶之規格要求。Uni-load mainly serves to meet local customers' specification requirements.
  • 主機板須以高精密設備製造，故向 ODM 採購；SSD、HDD 於當地採購具成本優勢。Mainboards require high-precision manufacturing and are sourced from the ODM; SSDs and HDDs are more cost-effective to source locally.
  • 澳洲常見警政單位標案，例如 ACA 之標案規模達兩萬台筆電。Police tenders are common in Australia; for example, an ACA tender covered 20,000 notebooks.

D1G2.B38485S6A7.0809110

    定義Definitions

    OBM Original Brand Manufacturer

    自有品牌經營。企業以自有品牌行銷與銷售產品，並主導產品定義；生產可自行或委託 ODM／OEM。本網站之例：Acer。The company markets and sells under its own brand and defines the product; production may be in-house or outsourced to an ODM or OEM. Example on this site: Acer.

    ODM Original Design Manufacturer

    委託設計製造。代工廠依品牌商之規格需求負責產品設計與生產，產品以品牌商之品牌銷售。本網站之例：仁寶、廣達、緯創、英業達、和碩。The manufacturer designs and builds the product to the brand's requirements, and the product is sold under the brand's name. Examples on this site: Compal, Quanta, Wistron, Inventec, Pegatron.

    OEM Original Equipment Manufacturer

    原廠委託製造。品牌商提供產品設計與規格，代工廠依設計生產，產品以品牌商之品牌銷售。The brand provides the design and specification; the manufacturer builds to that design, and the product is sold under the brand's name.

    EMS Electronics Manufacturing Services

    電子製造服務。受託進行電子產品之製造、組裝與測試，通常不負責產品設計，且同時服務多個品牌。本網站之例：鴻海。Contract manufacturing, assembly and testing of electronics, usually without product design, for many brands at once. Example on this site: Foxconn.

    用語提醒Note on usage

    台灣產業用語中，OEM 指依客戶設計代工生產；歐美 PC 產業中，「PC OEM」則常指 Dell、HP、Acer 等品牌電腦廠商本身。閱讀文件時應依上下文判斷。In Taiwan, OEM usually means building to a client's design; in the Western PC industry, "PC OEMs" often means the brands themselves, such as Dell, HP and Acer. Read the term in context.

    A computer passes through several kinds of company between design and repair. The roles below follow the flow of products and spare parts, with examples from this site.

    角色Role說明Meaning
    品牌商（OBM）Brand owner (OBM)定義產品、擁有品牌並負責行銷與售後；本網站之例為宏碁，其泛亞太區組織為 PAP。Defines the product, owns the brand and handles marketing and after-sales. Example: Acer, whose Pan Asia Pacific organisation is PAP.
    代工廠（ODM／EMS）Manufacturer (ODM / EMS)依品牌商規格設計與生產整機與料件；備品亦由代工廠供應。例：仁寶、廣達、緯創、英業達、和碩、鴻海。Designs and builds units and parts to the brand's specification; spare parts also come from the manufacturer. Examples: Compal, Quanta, Wistron, Inventec, Pegatron, Foxconn.

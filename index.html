標案與 Uni-loadTenders and Uni-load

標案作業方式，以及設置 Uni-load 之目的。

How tenders work and why Uni-load is set up.

標案流程Tender process

  1. 1客戶（如政府機關、警政單位）發布標書（規格書）。The customer (e.g. a government agency or the police) issues a tender with a specification.
  2. 2各品牌依規格書投標，產品須符合規格要求。Brands bid against the specification; products must meet its requirements.
  3. 3得標後依規格委託 ODM（如仁寶）生產成品（FG）。The winning brand has an ODM (e.g. Compal) build finished goods (FG) to the specification.
  4. 4成品成本高於標案價格時，可採澳洲模式：向 ODM 採購半成品（螢幕、主機板），於當地採購 SSD、HDD 並自行組裝。If the FG cost exceeds the tender price, the Australian approach applies: semi-finished parts (screen, mainboard) are purchased from the ODM, SSDs and HDDs are sourced locally, and units are assembled locally.
  5. 5藉此使成本符合標案價格。This brings the cost within the tender price.

Uni-load 的目的Purpose of Uni-load

  • Uni-load 主要用於滿足當地客戶之規格要求。Uni-load mainly serves to meet local customers' specification requirements.
  • 主機板須以高精密設備製造，故向 ODM 採購；SSD、HDD 於當地採購具成本優勢。Mainboards require high-precision manufacturing and are sourced from the ODM; SSDs and HDDs are more cost-effective to source locally.
  • 澳洲常見警政單位標案，例如 ACA 之標案規模達兩萬台筆電。Police tenders are common in Australia; for example, an ACA tender covered 20,000 notebooks.
  • AMI 為印尼 Uni-load 組裝廠之公司代碼。AMI is the code for the Indonesian Uni-load assembly company.

料號命名規則Part number naming rule

料號格式為 AA.BBBCD.EEE。字元位置不計小數點：第 6 碼為 RO 代碼，第 7 碼為國別代碼，前兩碼 AA 為子類別代碼。

Part numbers follow AA.BBBCD.EEE. Dots are not counted: the 6th character is the RO code, the 7th is the country code, and the first two characters (AA) are the sub-category code.

物流的基本概念What logistics is

物流（logistics）是把「對的東西」在「對的時間」、以「對的數量和狀態」送到「對的地點」所需的一連串活動。它不只是運送：事前的規劃、中間的倉儲與庫存管理、事後的回收都包含在內。

Logistics is the chain of activities that gets the right item to the right place, at the right time, in the right quantity and condition. It is more than transport: the planning before, the warehousing and inventory management in between, and the returns after are all part of it.

物流包含哪些活動What logistics covers

規劃與預測Planning and forecasting

估計未來需要多少、什麼時候需要、放在哪裡。estimating how much will be needed, when and where.

採購與下單Purchasing

向供應商下採購單，並追蹤出貨日期與交期。placing purchase orders with suppliers and tracking shipping dates and lead times.

運輸Transport

把貨物從供應商送到倉庫，再送到需要的地點。moving goods from the supplier to the warehouse and on to where they are needed.

倉儲Warehousing

收貨、上架、保管與揀貨出庫。receiving, put-away, storage and picking.

庫存管理Inventory management

決定每個地點要放多少存貨，既不缺貨也不囤積。deciding how much stock each location holds, avoiding both shortages and excess.

退貨與逆物流Returns and reverse logistics

把不良品或多餘的貨收回、維修、再利用或報廢。collecting defective or surplus goods for repair, reuse or scrapping.

物流要平衡的三件事The three things logistics balances

缺貨Shortages

存貨放太少，維修和出貨都要等；服務水準下降。Too little stock means waiting repairs and late shipments; the service level drops.

庫存成本Inventory cost

存貨放太多，積壓資金與倉儲空間，零件還可能過時。Too much stock ties up money and warehouse space, and parts can become obsolete.

交期Lead time

供應商出貨與運送要多久，決定了必須提前多久下單。How long the supplier and the transport take decides how far ahead an order must be placed.

物流的日常工作，就是在服務水準、庫存成本與交期之間取得平衡。Day-to-day logistics is the balance between service level, inventory cost and lead time.

服務物流是什麼What service logistics is

服務物流（售後服務物流）的任務，是讓每一件維修所需的備用零件在正確的時間、以正確的數量出現在維修據點，並把更換下來的不良品收回處理。本網站介紹的 PAP 服務物流團隊，就是在泛亞太區負責這件事的單位。

Service (after-sales) logistics makes sure every spare part a repair needs is at the service site at the right time and in the right quantity, and that the replaced defective parts are collected and dealt with. The PAP service logistics team this site describes is the unit responsible for this across the Pan Asia Pacific region.

與生產物流的差異How it differs from production logistics

生產物流Production logistics
  • 目的Purpose：把產品做出來並賣出去。: make the product and sell it.
  • 需求來源Demand：依生產計畫，可事先排定。: the production plan; it can be scheduled in advance.
  • 數量與種類Quantity and variety：大批量，種類相對集中。: large batches, relatively few items.
  • 時間要求Time pressure：依排程交貨。: delivery to a schedule.
  • 期間Duration：產品停產即結束。: ends when production stops.
  • 流向Direction：單向，零件 → 工廠 → 成品 → 客戶。: one way, parts → factory → product → customer.
服務物流Service logistics
  • 目的Purpose：讓已售出的產品能持續被維修、繼續使用。: keep products already sold repairable and in use.
  • 需求來源Demand：由故障發生決定，不確定、不規則。: failures; uncertain and irregular.
  • 數量與種類Quantity and variety：小批量，料號非常多（長尾）。: small quantities across a very long list of part numbers.
  • 時間要求Time pressure：維修在等料，就是客戶在等，講求速度。: a repair waiting for a part is a customer waiting; speed matters.
  • 期間Duration：停產後仍須供應多年，因此要在停產前做最後採購。: continues for years after production ends, so a last buy is made before it stops.
  • 流向Direction：雙向，良品送出、不良品收回。: two ways, good parts out and defective parts back.

備用零件的特性Characteristics of spare parts

備用零件（備品）是為了維修而準備的零件。它的需求和成品很不一樣：不確定、數量小、種類多，而且要在產品停產後持續供應好幾年。這些特性決定了服務物流的做法。

Spare parts are the parts kept for repairs. Their demand is unlike that for finished products: uncertain, small in quantity, very wide in variety, and it continues for years after a product stops being made. These characteristics shape how service logistics works.

需求特性Demand characteristics

間歇且不規則Intermittent and irregular

需求來自故障，受故障率與市場上仍在使用的機器數量影響，無法像生產一樣排定。demand comes from failures and depends on failure rates and how many units are still in use; it cannot be scheduled like production.

長尾A long tail

料號數量非常多，但大多數料號一年只用到幾件。there are a great many part numbers, most of which are used only a few times a year.

時間壓力Time pressure

缺一個零件，一台機器就修不好；待料（HFP）的時間直接影響客戶滿意度（CSAT）。one missing part stops a repair; time spent waiting for parts (HFP) directly affects customer satisfaction (CSAT).

生命週期長A long life

產品停產（EOM）後仍要供應維修用料，因此有最後採購（LTB）與停產後採購（PPB1/2）的機制。parts must still be supplied after production ends (EOM), which is why there are last-time buys (LTB) and post-production buys (PPB1/2).

替代料Alternates

同一個位置可能有多個可互換的料號，須在 Service BOM 中維護。one position may have several interchangeable part numbers, which must be maintained in the Service BOM.

雙向流動Two-way flow

良品送到據點，更換下來的不良品依 RMA 流程退回。good parts go out to the sites and the replaced defective parts come back under the RMA process.

價值差異大A wide range of value

從螺絲到主機板都是備品，規劃時依類別分別處理。anything from a screw to a mainboard is a spare part, so planning treats the classes differently.

一個重要觀念：FRUA key concept: the FRU

FRU（Field Replaceable Unit，現場可更換單元）是維修時直接整個換掉的單位，例如鍵盤模組或螢幕模組，而不是模組裡的單一電子元件。備品清單（SPL）列出的就是這些單位，備品規劃也以 FRU 為單位進行。A FRU (field replaceable unit) is the unit swapped as a whole during a repair, such as a keyboard module or a display module, rather than a single component inside it. The spare parts list (SPL) is a list of these units, and spare-parts planning works at the FRU level.

定義Definitions

OBM Original Brand Manufacturer

自有品牌經營。企業以自有品牌行銷與銷售產品，並主導產品定義；生產可自行或委託 ODM／OEM。本網站之例：Acer。The company markets and sells under its own brand and defines the product; production may be in-house or outsourced to an ODM or OEM. Example on this site: Acer.

ODM Original Design Manufacturer

委託設計製造。代工廠依品牌商之規格需求負責產品設計與生產，產品以品牌商之品牌銷售。本網站之例：仁寶、廣達、緯創、英業達、和碩。The manufacturer designs and builds the product to the brand's requirements, and the product is sold under the brand's name. Examples on this site: Compal, Quanta, Wistron, Inventec, Pegatron.

OEM Original Equipment Manufacturer

原廠委託製造。品牌商提供產品設計與規格，代工廠依設計生產，產品以品牌商之品牌銷售。The brand provides the design and specification; the manufacturer builds to that design, and the product is sold under the brand's name.

EMS Electronics Manufacturing Services

電子製造服務。受託進行電子產品之製造、組裝與測試，通常不負責產品設計，且同時服務多個品牌。本網站之例：鴻海。Contract manufacturing, assembly and testing of electronics, usually without product design, for many brands at once. Example on this site: Foxconn.

備品供應流程的基本概念The basic supply flow for spare parts

不論哪個品牌，備品供應都繞著同一條線運作：先估需求，再向供應商採購，進倉保管，依各地需求送出，最後在維修現場換上，並把換下來的不良品收回。Whatever the brand, spare-parts supply runs along the same line: estimate demand, buy from the supplier, hold the stock in a warehouse, ship it to where it is needed, fit it during the repair, and collect the defective part that was replaced.

1估需求Estimate demand

依維修量與故障率估計各地要多少備品。Estimate how many parts each location will need from repair volumes and failure rates.

2採購Buy

向供應商下採購單，追蹤出貨與交期。Place orders with the supplier and track shipping and lead time.

3倉儲Store

收貨、入庫、保管並掌握庫存數量。Receive, put away, hold the stock and keep track of quantities.

4供應Supply

依各地需求把備品送到服務據點。Ship parts to the service sites as they need them.

5維修Repair

維修人員換上備品，完成維修。The technician fits the part and completes the repair.

6回收Return

換下來的不良品收回檢查、維修或報廢。The replaced defective part is collected for inspection, repair or scrapping.

標案流程Tender process

1發布標書Tender issued

客戶（如政府機關、警政單位）發布標書（規格書）。The customer (e.g. a government agency or the police) issues a tender with a specification.

2依規格投標Bids against the spec

各品牌依規格書投標，產品須符合規格要求。Brands bid against the specification; products must meet its requirements.

3委託 ODM 生產ODM builds the units

得標後依規格委託 ODM（如仁寶 Compal）生產成品（FG）。The winning brand has an ODM (e.g. Compal) build finished goods (FG) to the specification.

4澳洲模式：當地組裝Australian approach: local assembly

成品成本高於標案價格時，可採澳洲模式：向 ODM 採購半成品（螢幕、主機板），於當地採購 SSD、HDD 並自行組裝。If the FG cost exceeds the tender price, the Australian approach applies: semi-finished parts (screen, mainboard) are purchased from the ODM, SSDs and HDDs are sourced locally, and units are assembled locally.

5符合標案價格Within the tender price

藉此使成本符合標案價格。This brings the cost within the tender price.

Uni-load 的目的Purpose of Uni-load

滿足當地規格Meet local specifications

Uni-load 主要用於滿足當地客戶之規格要求。Uni-load mainly serves to meet local customers' specification requirements.

分工採購Split sourcing

主機板須以高精密設備製造，故向 ODM 採購；SSD、HDD 於當地採購具成本優勢。Mainboards require high-precision manufacturing and are sourced from the ODM; SSDs and HDDs are more cost-effective to source locally.

第 6 碼與第 7 碼怎麼看How to read characters 6 and 7

D1G2.B38485S6A7.0809110

  2. 第 6 碼＝區域代碼：S 為 AAP（亞太），T 為 TWN（台灣），C 為 ACCN（中國），E 為 EMEA，A 為 PA（美洲）。本例第 6 碼為 S，即 AAP 區之料號。Character 6 = which region. S is AAP (Asia Pacific), T is TWN (Taiwan), C is ACCN (China), E is EMEA and A is PA (Americas). The S here means this part number belongs to the AAP region.
  3. 第 7 碼＝該區域內之國別代碼，僅於第 6 碼為 S（AAP）時適用：A 為 ACA（澳洲），M 為 ASSB（馬來西亞），I 為 AIL（印度），完整對照見下表。本例第 7 碼為 A，即澳洲。Character 7 = which country in that region.     名詞Term說明Meaning
    保固內／保固外（OOW）In warranty / out of warranty (OOW)保固期內之維修由品牌商負擔；保固外（OOW）維修由客戶付費，海柏特即以保固外維修為主要業務。Repairs in the warranty period are borne by the brand; out-of-warranty (OOW) repairs are paid by the customer and are HSN's main business.

    名詞Term說明Meaning
    備品（S/P、Spare Parts）Spare parts (S/P)供維修更換之料件；PAP 負責備品之規劃、採購與倉儲。Parts kept for repair replacement; PAP plans, purchases and stores them.

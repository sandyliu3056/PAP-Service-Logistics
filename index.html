泛亞太區（PAP）的服務物流團隊，夾在各國子公司（NS）與總部（HQ）之間：規劃備品、下單、監控庫存，並維護 Service BOM 與料號。這裡把內部訓練教材整理成可以切換中英文、可以搜尋的網頁。

The Pan Asia Pacific (PAP) service logistics team sits between the nation subsidiaries (NS) and headquarters (HQ). It plans spare parts, places orders, monitors inventory, and maintains the Service BOM and part numbers. This site turns the internal training deck into searchable pages in Chinese and English.

料號命名規則Part number naming ruleACER P/N Naming Rule

料號格式為 AA.BBBCD.EEE。不算小數點，第 6 碼是 RO 代碼，第 7 碼是國別代碼；開頭兩碼 AA 對應子類別。

Part numbers follow AA.BBBCD.EEE. Not counting the dots, the 6th character is the RO code and the 7th is the country code; the first two characters (AA) give the sub-category.

第 6 碼 · RO 代碼6th character · RO code

第 7 碼 · 國別代碼（AAP）7th character · country code (AAP)
